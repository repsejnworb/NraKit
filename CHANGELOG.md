# NraKit 0.29.0

### New
- Auctionator prices (needs Auctionator): what things cost at the auction house, from Auctionator's last scan. A Waylaid Crate's tooltip prices each bundle it takes, with the cheapest in green, and an Auctionator shopping list, "Waylaid Crates", holds every bundle of the crates in your bags. A recipe's tooltip shows what one craft costs, how many of each reagent you have (green in your bags, yellow elsewhere, with which of your characters has them) and what's left to buy; the crates show what you have too. Right-click a recipe link in chat or a recipe in your profession window to make an Auctionator shopping list of its reagents, or to add them to the NraKit Shopping List: a new tab (/nra shop) with everything you're crafting, its amounts (change them), prices, a total and how many you have on all your characters, which Auctionator gets a copy of. Reagents sold by vendors show the vendor's price (NraKit remembers them from merchants you visit) and are left out of the auction house search.

### Fixed
- NraKit's messages in the middle of the screen are only as wide as what they show, so the mouse no longer holds one up (or clicks it) from far beside its text.

Type /nra new in game to see what's new in every version.
