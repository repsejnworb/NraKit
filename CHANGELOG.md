# NraKit 0.19.0

### New
- /nra preview: see any of NraKit's on-screen messages with made-up content (goal complete, milestones, quest reminders, Unit Scanner alerts, player warnings, the talent reminder, the campfire bar), to check how they look. /nra preview lists them.
- Quests to turn in: /nra done (or a key) shows a small list of your finished quests by the zone you turn them in. Click one to see it on the map, right-click to make it your waypoint. The zones are sorted by how far away they are, and entering one where you have quests to turn in shows a small message you can click to open the list.
- Mail: an arrow next to Open All with more ways to open: collect only auction gold (sales and outbid refunds), auction purchases or expired auctions, or take the money from every mail and leave the items. Each says how many mails and how much gold first.
- Mail: Alt+click an item in your bags with Send Mail open to attach every stack of it you have (all your Silk Cloth, all your Small Red Pouches), as many as fit.

### Improved
- Mail: Open selected mail, Fill in mail subject and Remember mail recipient are now parts of one Mail feature, each with its own switch. If you had any of them on, Mail is on with the same parts.

Type /nra new in game to see what's new in every version.
