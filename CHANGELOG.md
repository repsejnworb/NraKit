# NraKit 0.24.0

### New
- Unlearned abilities: after a respec, abilities you don't know anymore stay on your bars, only greyed out. Now they get a red cross and a tooltip line, and when your spells change a message says which ones on your bars you lost (click it to take them off, after combat if you're fighting) and which new ones aren't on your bars yet. /nra unlearned lists them, /nra unlearned remove takes them off. /nra preview unlearned shows the message.
- Wishlist: a list of gear you want, per character, by slot. Add items with /nra wishlist <item> [note] (shift-click it in; /nra wish for short), by right-clicking an item link in chat or an item in Item lookup, or by dropping them on the new Wishlist tab. Each shows the level to wear it and your note on where it comes from; filter by Ready, Not yet or Got. Items you get are ticked off with a message. A star marks wishlist items in your bags, at vendors, in quest rewards, the loot window and loot rolls (gold while you want it, grey once you have it), and item tooltips say they're on your wishlist.

### Fixed
- Copy Wowhead link: the box with the link now shows on top of NraKit's windows (it was hidden behind them when copying an item from Item lookup).

Type /nra new in game to see what's new in every version.
