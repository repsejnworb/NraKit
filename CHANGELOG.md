# NraKit 0.26.0

### New
- Reminders & to-dos: notes to yourself that come back when you need them. /nra remind go to Darkshore pops up at your next login; /nra todo respec just goes on your to-do list. Put a trigger in front for later: a time (30m, 2h, 1d), a zone (in Ironforge: ..., straight away if you're there), a level (at 30: ...) or another of your characters (@Bankalt: ...). Reminders pop up on a card that stays until you close it and says why it came up, with a sound you can pick. The list on screen holds your reminders and to-dos until you tick them, in your order (drag or use the arrows), with tags for where, when and who from. Zone to-dos are there while you're in their zone: leave and a short countdown takes them off, return and they're back (with a small message for arriving); fold it, and pick its size, background (also a stronger one while the mouse is over it), which way it grows, whether it shows its header and whether it hides in combat. The Reminders & To-do tab lists everything, with a form for new ones and for changing any of them (right-click, Edit). /nra preview reminder shows the card.
- NraKit button: NraKit's icon, anywhere on your screen. A badge counts what you may have missed (reminders you haven't ticked off, and what's new if you like), and the tooltip says what. Pick what a click and a shift-click do; right-click opens NraKit's menu. Lock it, size it, or hide it in combat.

### Improved
- Quests to turn in: the message when you enter a zone lists the quests one per line (click one to open the list), shares one message with other things for you there (like to-dos), and isn't shown in combat.
- NraKit's messages in the middle of the screen stack: up to three at once instead of one at a time. The newest pops up at the top, always in the same place, and pushes the others down (a goal done no longer pushes them away). More wait their turn, and when one goes, the older ones slide up. A message stays while the mouse is on it, and the same one isn't shown twice (zoning in and out keeps the one up).
- Wishlist: the ? button at the top shows how to add items again (with examples), once your wishlist isn't empty anymore.
- Unlearned abilities: clicking the message asks before it takes anything off your action bars, naming the abilities.

### Fixed
- Unlearned abilities: the message after a respec waits until you close the talents window, instead of sitting on top of it where a click meant for the window could take abilities off your bars.

Type /nra new in game to see what's new in every version.
